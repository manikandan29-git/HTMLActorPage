vijay

He is an indian actor and he left from the acting and started to work as a politician. He is the current chief minister of Tamil Nadu. He is the founder of the political party called "Makkal Needhi Maiam". He is the son of the famous actor and politician M.G.R. He has acted in many Tamil movies and has won several awards for his performances. He is also known for his philanthropic work and has been involved in various charitable activities.
